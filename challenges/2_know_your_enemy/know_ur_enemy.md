## Description

> Understanding why we do things is just as important as knowing how to do them.
>
> ACFC's Red Team uses TTPs that closely align with APT groups and state-sponsored attackers when simulating attack scenarios.
>
> What is the PRIMARY reason for simulating these specific threat actors rather than using generic attack scenarios?
>
> * Because APT attacks are the only category of threat relevant to telecommunications companies and their infrastructure
> * To produce more comprehensive and technically detailed findings reports that demonstrate higher value to senior leadership
> * To test whether the organisation's defences hold against the most sophisticated and realistic threats it is likely to face in the real world
> * Because generic attack scenarios do not satisfy the technical requirements set out by the CREST accreditation framework
