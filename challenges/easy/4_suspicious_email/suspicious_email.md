# Suspicious Transmission

**Category:** Open-Source & Entry Clues
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Points:** 100
**Files:** `URGENT MISSION CONTROL.eml`

---

## Description

> A crew member reports receiving a suspicious message claiming to be from Mission Control requesting credential verification. Analyze the email header to determine the true origin of the message.
>
> Flag Format: `ACFC{...}`

---


No tools beyond a text editor are required for this challenge.
The key insight is knowing where to look, and knowing that email clients hide the most important forensic information by default.
